iSchool Drop-In Advising Analytics: Autumn 2025
Welcome to the Autumn 2025 iSchool Drop-In Advising Visualization!

These visualizations were created by Diego Licea using P5.js. They work off template code from the INFO 474 Autumn 2025 Final with professor Belén Saldías.

The data used in these visualizations were collected over the course of the Autumn 2025 quarter during the days iSchool drop-in advising was available.

I hope to update this site with the ability to view future quarter analytics as well as add additional visualizations.
Drop-in Advising Analysis - Pt. 1/2
In autumn 2025, we had a total of 493 total signups for drop-in advising meetings

Of those signups, 278 of them were "Completed" while 215 were "Incomplete" for a 56.4% meeting completion rate. Meetings were marked "Complete" based on whether they had either an assigned helping advisor or a completion time.

We saw a generally downward leaning trend in meetings up until the beginning of November, where meetings spiked as Winter 2026 registration opened then dropped as the quarter ended.
Drop-in Advising Analysis - Pt. 2/2
The biggest topic students came in to talk about was Degree Requirements & Planning This topic was followed by Graduation and Registration.

The majority of students who came in for drop-ins were Current INFO Majors, with Prospective INFO Majors making up a large minority.

It'll be interesting to see whether topics/statuses change as the year goes on, factoring in things like the new registration cycle, students graduating in the winter/spring, etc.
